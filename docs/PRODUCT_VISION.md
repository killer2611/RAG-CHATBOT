# Product Vision — Flagship RAG Knowledge Platform

## Product
A production-oriented AI/RAG knowledge platform that lets users ingest documents, ask grounded questions, inspect evidence, and evaluate retrieval/generation quality.

## Core promise
**Ask your knowledge base. See the evidence. Measure the intelligence.**

## Real capabilities
- PDF/TXT/DOCX ingestion
- persistent document indexing
- conversational RAG
- history-aware question reformulation
- dense retrieval
- parent/child hierarchical context
- BGE cross-encoder reranking
- grounded answering with abstention
- persistent sessions
- asynchronous DeepEval evaluation
- Ollama/Gemini evaluation judges
- health/status endpoint

## Product pillars
1. Knowledge — documents become searchable knowledge.
2. Retrieval — evidence is found and ranked.
3. Grounding — answers are constrained by supplied evidence.
4. Evaluation — system quality is measured rather than assumed.

## Experience principles
The UI should make the intelligence of the system visible without faking internal behavior.

The flagship surfaces are:
- Home / showcase
- Chat workspace
- Evidence inspector
- Knowledge library
- Evaluation lab
- System observability
- Settings

## Design philosophy
Premium, technical, calm, precise, original.

Quality references:
- Vercel-like restraint
- Linear-like information hierarchy
- Raycast-like interaction quality
- Perplexity-like evidence clarity

Do not copy another product.

## Explicitly avoid
- generic AI landing-page templates;
- fake benchmark numbers;
- fake agent traces;
- fake confidence;
- fake telemetry;
- decorative complexity that harms performance;
- giant dependency collections.

## Trust
The product should visibly communicate why an answer was produced.
Evidence is more important than theatrical “AI magic.”

## Success
A technically sophisticated engineer should recognize the retrieval/evaluation architecture.
A non-technical user should still understand the product immediately.
The interface should feel like a serious AI product rather than a student chatbot.
